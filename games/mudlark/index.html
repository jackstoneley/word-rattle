How to play
  • Press Slide and Buddy slides down the mud and flies off the lip.
  • In the air, hold ▲ to lift the nose and ▼ to drop it. With wings, a nose just a little above the way you're going glides furthest. Too high and you stall.
  • With a booster, hold Boost to fly where the nose points until the fuel runs out.
  • Catch the floating bone treats. Every flight pays treats for distance, height, speed and treats caught, plus bonuses for goals.
  • Spend treats in the kit shop between flights. The lady dogs are waiting in the dog park, 3 km from the slope: land next to them to finish. Overshoot and Buddy panics. You can keep flying after that for a longer best.
