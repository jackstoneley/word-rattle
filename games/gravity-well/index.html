Gravity Well

Launch the ball. Let the planets bend it to the target.

Levels

How to play
  • Get the white ball into the green target. It is usually out of view: the green arrow at the edge of the screen points to it and says how far away it is.
  • Drag anywhere on space to swing the aiming arrow round and make it longer or shorter. Longer is a harder shot. For fine aiming, use the ↺ ↻ buttons (a tenth of a degree) and − + (one percent of power).
  • Tap Launch. Every planet pulls on the ball, harder the closer it gets, and several planets pull at once. Pink planets push it away instead.
  • The dashed rings round a planet show where its pull is strong. Moons go round their planet once you launch, and start from the same place every shot.
  • Hit a planet, fly off the edge of space (the faint dashed box) or loop for too long and the shot is over. Your last two shots stay drawn so you can adjust.
  • Map zooms out to the whole level. One shot earns three stars, up to three shots earns two.
