Boxed In

Hold out against the zombie boxes. Your gun aims itself.

Arenas

How to play
  • Put your thumb anywhere on the left of the screen and drag to walk.
  • Your gun aims at the nearest zombie you can see and fires by itself. Keep moving and keep them in front of you.
  • Your score unlocks new weapons as you go: uzi, shotgun, barrels, grenades, barricades, then rockets. Tap one at the bottom right to switch.
  • Barrels and barricades are placed, not fired: pick one and tap Place. Shoot a barrel to blow it up. Zombies have to smash through barricades.
  • Quick kills in a row build a combo that multiplies your score.
  • Red devils appear from wave 4 and throw fireballs. Crates bring health and ammo. Watch out: your own explosions hurt you too. Stay out of trouble for a few seconds and you slowly heal.
  • Survive wave 8 in an arena to open the next one.
