How to play
  • Draw with your finger, then press Play. Buddy slides down whatever you draw. Press Stop to change things and try again.
  • Track lines are what Buddy rides on. Boost lines (orange) speed him up the way you drew them. Scenery lines (green) are just for show.
  • Buddy lands on the top of a line. Draw lines left to right, and a loop in one go, anticlockwise.
  • Tools: pencil to draw freehand, ruler for straight lines, rubber to rub out, hand to move about. Pinch with two fingers to zoom.
  • Challenges: reach the flag for a star, catch every bone for another, and use no more ink than par for the third. You can't draw on flowerbeds, and thorns knock Buddy off.
  • Stuck? Tap ⋯ and Show the answer for a faint example track.
  • Your own tracks: tap Send to share one as a link. Whoever taps it gets your track to watch and change.
