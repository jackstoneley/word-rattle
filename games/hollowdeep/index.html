How to play
  • The glowing orb is your Heart. If it is destroyed you lose. Destroy every rival Heart to win.
  • ⛏ Dig: tap rock to mark it. To mark lots, press and hold, then drag. Your diggers dig marked rock when they are free, and claim the new floor for you. Yellow rock is gold.
  • 🧱 Build: rooms go on your own coloured floor. Lair gives beds, Mushroom farm gives food, Treasury holds gold, Training pit and Library level creatures up and bring new kinds.
  • Shapes matter. A 2×2 square of one room makes something special: a Book stand in a Library, a Vault in a Treasury, a Big nest in a Lair. A 3×3 square of Training pit, Library, Workshop or Shrine makes something grander. Tap a room to see what its shapes do.
  • Workshop: plan doors and traps from 🧱 Build. Tinkers and spare diggers make them, then a digger fits them. Doors let your creatures through but rivals must smash them. A Workbench (2×2) and a Great forge (3×3) unlock better ones.
  • Creatures come through a Gate you own. Dig a tunnel to the nearest Gate, and your diggers will claim it.
  • Creatures look after themselves: they sleep, eat and train. Every 2½ minutes is payday. Unpaid, hungry or bedless creatures get cross and leave.
  • ✨ Spells use mana, which flows in faster the more floor you own. Lightning and Possess are ready at the start; the Library researches the rest (Heal, Sight, Haste, Call to arms, Cave-in).
  • 👑 Possess a creature to steer it yourself with the thumb stick, and hold ⚔ to fight. It hits half as hard again, but uses mana while you hold on.
  • Prisoners: with a Jail, enemies your side beats are knocked out instead of killed, and your diggers lock them up. A Persuasion room wins them over to your side: heroes and wild beasts too.
  • Out in the dark: lost creatures (with a ?) join whoever reaches them first. Wild beasts attack anything near their nest. Sparkling rock hides treasure. Lava can't be crossed; water slows everyone down.
  • Old places: from the second realm on, a few old places lie hidden in the rock, about halfway between you and your rivals. A Sealed tomb has guardians: beat them, let a digger claim it, and its treasure is yours. A Mana spring, War idol, Mother lode or Healing well helps whoever owns the floor under it, so rivals will try to take it.
  • Surprises: every few minutes something happens. A tremor opens a new tunnel, a rockfall bares a gold seam, beasts burst into somebody's realm, or sell-swords camp in the rock for whoever reaches them first.
  • Who is welcome: tap 👾 at the top to choose which kinds may come through your Gates. ★ Rare creatures come only when something unusual is true: a Shade (a spy who steals rival gold) wants a Watchtower and a rival's Heart found; a Wyrm wants a Vault and 4,000 gold; a Ghoul a Crypt and six of your own dead; a Sporeling a huge farm; an Ember imp floor beside lava.
  • Heroes come down the stairs from the surface in waves, with a Dwarf digging the way. Doors, traps and a strong guard keep them out.
  • 🚩 Flag: plant it, and your creatures march there and fight anything on the way. Plant it on a rival's Heart to attack it.
  • ✋ Hand: pick up as many as 8 creatures, then tap your own floor to drop them there.
  • 👆 Look: tap anything to see what it is doing. Tap the 👾 count for your whole realm.
  • Drag to look around, and pinch to zoom. Tap the little map to jump. The game saves itself.
