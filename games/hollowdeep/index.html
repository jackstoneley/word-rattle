How to play
  • The glowing orb is your Heart. If it is destroyed you lose. Destroy every rival Heart to win.
  • ⛏ Dig: tap rock to mark it. To mark lots, press and hold, then drag. Your diggers dig marked rock when they are free, and claim the new floor for you. Yellow rock is gold.
  • 🧱 Build: rooms go on your own coloured floor. Lair gives beds, Mushroom farm gives food, Treasury holds gold, Training pit and Library level creatures up and bring new kinds.
  • Creatures come through a Gate you own. Dig a tunnel to the nearest Gate, and your diggers will claim it.
  • Creatures look after themselves: they sleep, eat and train. Every 2½ minutes is payday. Unpaid, hungry or bedless creatures get cross and leave.
  • 🚩 Flag: plant it, and your creatures march there and fight anything on the way. Plant it on a rival's Heart to attack it.
  • ✋ Hand: pick up as many as 8 creatures, then tap your own floor to drop them there.
  • 👆 Look: tap anything to see what it is doing. Tap the 👾 count for your whole realm.
  • Drag to look around, and pinch to zoom. Tap the little map to jump. The game saves itself.
