Lee Lee's Kitchen

Take orders, cook, serve and clean up. Grow a little café or a busy pub into the best in town.

Campaign

25 days that teach you everything, from a corner café to a restaurant.

Free play

Run your own place, day after day. Five save slots.

How to play
  • Tap anything and Lee Lee walks over and uses it. She carries one thing at a time.
  • Not sure what to do? Follow the green arrow: it points at the next useful thing (change it in Settings).
  • Customers sit down. When a table shows a notepad, tap it to take their order. The order appears on the strip at the top.
  • Tap the station a dish starts at (usually the fridge). Then follow the glowing stations: chop, mix, cook. Food on the toaster, grill, hob, fryer and oven burns if you leave it too long.
  • Finished food is plated up by tapping the plates 🍽 on the counter, then tap the table to serve it.
  • Tap an order on the strip at the top and Lee Lee goes to start it (or serves it, if she's holding it).
  • Keep tables happy one after another for a 🔥 streak: every happy table in a row adds to the tips.
  • Hands full? Put anything down on one of the counter spots or a worktop, and tap it again to pick it up.
  • When people leave, tap the table to clear the dirty plates and take them to the sink. No clean plates, no food!
  • Customers who wait too long walk out without paying, and your reputation drops. Happy customers tip and bring more people.
  • In a pub, late nights bring rowdy customers (red face). Tap their table to calm them; the second time they're shown the door. Mop up any spills.
  • In free play each day has two goals worth a bonus, and you can choose how busy it gets: Relaxed, Normal or Busy.
  • After each day you get a tip for next time, then you're in the office: learn recipes, hire and promote staff, buy equipment and decor, advertise, and one day move somewhere bigger. Stock is topped up for you (you can turn that off).
  • New to it? Start with the campaign: each day teaches one thing, from a café to a restaurant. Stuck on a day? After two tries you can have an easier go. Finishing a chapter earns a certificate worth £75 in free play.
  • The game saves at the end of each day. Leaving in the middle of a day starts that day again.
