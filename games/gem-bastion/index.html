How to play
  • Tap a gem colour in the forge row to see what it does, then press Forge. The gem goes into your pouch, or straight into a socket if you tapped an empty one first.
  • Drag a gem from your pouch onto a socket on the map. You can also tap the gem, then tap the socket.
  • Drag one gem onto another to merge. Two gems of the same grade make one gem a grade higher. Mixed gems keep most of each colour's power.
  • A gem's two strongest colours unlock a combo, such as Ruby + Emerald for Toxic Splash. Every pair of colours has one, listed below.
  • Grades stop at 10. Merge two grade 10 gems to make a Super gem. Pick one of two paths, then spend coins to raise it to level 3.
  • Each boss you beat lets you pick a relic, a perk for the rest of the run.
  • Traps are the diamond sockets on the path. Their hits are weak, but their powers are twice as strong.
  • Every 10th wave is a boss. Bosses drop shards even if you lose the run. Spend shards in the Forge.
  • Call the next wave early for bonus coins. Waves overlap, so do not get greedy.
  • Rage (button under the map) makes every coming wave tougher. Raging waves pay more coins, and each raging kill earns part of a shard.
  • You start with Ruby only. Spend shards in Gems to unlock more colours and to master the ones you have.
  • Handicaps open once you clear the first map. Each level makes runs harder and adds 15% to every shard you earn.
