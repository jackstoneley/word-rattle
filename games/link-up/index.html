How to play

Find four groups of four words that share something. Tap four words, then Submit. Get a group right and it locks in at the top.

You can make four mistakes. If three of your four words belong together, you'll see One away.

Watch for red herrings: a word can look like it fits two groups, but only one answer works for all sixteen.
