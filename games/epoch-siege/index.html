How to play
  • Your base is on the left, the enemy's on the right. Knock theirs down before they knock down yours.
  • Army: tap a unit to train it (up to five wait in the line). Fighters hold the front, shooters stand behind them, heavies are slow and tough, healers patch up the troops near them.
  • You earn gold all the time and more for every enemy you beat. Beating enemies also gives experience: fill the bar and tap Evolve to move into the next age, with far stronger units, defences and a stronger base.
  • Defences sit on your base and shoot whatever comes close. Buy more spots for them, and sell old ones when you move up an age.
  • Upgrades last the whole battle: sharper weapons, tougher troops, thicker walls, more gold, better defences and faster training.
  • The special button rains damage on the enemy army (in the Middle Ages it's a war horn that makes your troops hit harder and walk faster). It needs time to recharge.
  • The orders button switches between Charge, Hold (stand a little way out from your base) and Fall back (come home). Holding is handy while you save up.
  • In the Future Age there's nowhere left to evolve: your experience charges the Star Lance instead, which knocks a fifth off the enemy base. The enemy has one too, so keep beating their troops.
  • Each age has a champion: expensive, and only one at a time, but it is tough and hits bases three times as hard. Champions are how you break through strong defences.
  • A unit that beats three enemies becomes a veteran (gold star): it heals up and hits harder.
  • Drag the battlefield to look around, or tap the strip at the top to jump there. The battle saves itself, so you can leave and carry on later.
